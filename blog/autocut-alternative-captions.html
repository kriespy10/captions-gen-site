Updated 8 November 2026 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

If captions are all you need, you don't have to pay for a whole editing suite. Here's how AutoCut compares with a free alternative for captions.

What AutoCut is

AutoCut is a set of AI editing tools that run inside Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. The main ones:

  • AutoCut Silences: removes pauses and dead air.
  • AutoPodcast: switches between podcast cameras by who's talking.
  • AutoZoom: adds punch-in zooms.
  • AutoCaptions: adds animated captions.

It's a strong tool if you use several of those. If you only want captions, you're paying for a lot you don't use.

What AutoCut captions cost

AutoCut lists a Basic plan for silence removal and an AI Plan for its wider editing features, including captions. It advertises a 14-day trial with no credit card. Check the current pricing page for the quote, billing period and taxes that apply to you.

Captions Gen is currently free, with no subscription. Its download uses an email checkout, with no payment card. Premiere Pro and After Effects are separate paid Adobe applications, and paid Captions Gen features are planned for the future.

When AutoCut is the better pick

  • You also want silence cutting, zooms or podcast camera switching.
  • You edit on a Mac or in DaVinci Resolve.
  • You already pay for the AI Plan. Try its captions before adding another tool.

When Captions Gen is the better pick

  • You only need captions. No subscription for one feature.
  • You need local transcription. Captions Gen doesn't upload your audio or video for transcription.
  • You reuse one style. Save your look and choose Make main preset from its tile menu.
  • You want to restyle later. Pick another preset and apply it to captions you already placed.

How to switch

Try a short copy of a finished sequence first. Initial setup time includes downloading the speech models.

  1. Get the free Windows setup through the email checkout, with no payment card. Save your work, close the Adobe apps, then choose Premiere Pro in setup. You need Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, Creative Cloud Desktop and Adobe installation permissions.
  2. Reopen Premiere Pro and go to Window > UXP Plugins > Captions Gen. Let any missing speech models download before generating; allow at least 3 GB for the built-in models. Later transcription uses the cached models on your CPU.
  3. Select the clip with the voice in your timeline.

Use the full Setup for a first installation. Check the current product requirements and 1.1.9 release notes if you're updating an existing installation.

  1. Pick a look on the Presets tab.
  2. Change colors or size on Customize if you like.
  3. Press Generate.

Using both together

A workflow to test is finishing the cut with AutoCut, then adding captions with Captions Gen. Keep a project copy; this isn't a compatibility guarantee for every combination of plugin versions.

  1. Cut your video with AutoCut as usual.
  2. Select the final clip and generate captions with Captions Gen.

Tip: Add captions last. If you change the cut afterwards, generate again so the timing stays right.

Questions people ask

Is AutoCut free?

AutoCut advertises a 14-day free trial without a credit card. Continued AutoCaptions use needs a paid plan; check the current AI Plan quote and billing period on its pricing page. Vendor pages reviewed 8 November 2026.

What is a free AutoCut alternative for captions?

For Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows, Captions Gen is currently free without a time limit or watermark. It creates animated Motion Graphics clips using local CPU transcription. It doesn't cut silences or switch podcast cameras.

Is there a free AutoCut alternative for DaVinci Resolve or Mac?

Captions Gen's public installer is for Premiere Pro and After Effects on Windows. It doesn't include DaVinci Resolve support or a Mac installer. For ordinary subtitles on Mac, check the native caption tools in the editor you already use.

Can I use AutoCut and Captions Gen together?

You can try finishing the cut in AutoCut before generating captions with Captions Gen. Keep a project copy and test a short section first; this guide does not establish compatibility between every version or feature of the two plugins.

Does Captions Gen upload my footage?

Captions Gen doesn't upload audio or video for transcription. It uses local CPU models; downloading missing models and checking for updates still need an internet connection.
