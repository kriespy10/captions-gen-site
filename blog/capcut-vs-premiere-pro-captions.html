CapCut vs Premiere Pro captions

Updated 2026-08-26 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

CapCut wins on caption style, Premiere wins on everything else about editing. That's why so many editors round-trip: edit in Premiere, caption in CapCut, bring it back. A caption plugin removes the reason for the trip.

Short on time? Captions Gen does animated karaoke captions in Premiere Pro in one click. Try it free (15 minutes of transcription included).

Styling and animation

CapCut has dozens of animated caption templates with word highlighting, built for TikTok. Premiere's caption track has one static style per track and no animation. On looks alone, CapCut is ahead.

Accuracy

Both are reasonable on clean speech. Premiere transcribes locally, CapCut in the cloud. Names and technical words need fixing in either.

Editing after captioning

In Premiere, captions are clips you can retime, restyle and correct at any point. CapCut's captions come back as burned-in pixels, so a typo means redoing the trip.

Privacy

CapCut uploads your footage. Premiere doesn't. For client work under NDA, that decides it.

Time per revision

The round trip costs an export, a caption pass, another export and an import, every time the edit changes. Plugins that generate animated captions inside Premiere cost none of that.

Getting the CapCut look in Premiere

A plugin like Captions Gen generates the highlighted-word style as native graphics on your timeline. Same look, no export loop, and captions you can still edit.
