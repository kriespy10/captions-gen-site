A free FireCut alternative for captions

Updated 22 October 2026 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

FireCut is an AI editing plugin for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. It removes silences, adds zooms and chapters, and makes captions. The captions come with its paid Pro plan.

If captions are the main thing you use, here's a free way to get them in Premiere Pro, and an honest look at when FireCut is still the better buy.

In short

  • FireCut's Premiere Pro captions are on the Pro plan: $34 a month, or $20 a month billed yearly.
  • Pro includes 25 hours of transcription a month.
  • Captions Gen makes animated, word-by-word captions in Premiere Pro for free, with no hour limit.
  • FireCut does much more than captions. Captions Gen is captions only, on Windows.

What FireCut captions cost

For Premiere Pro, FireCut lists three plans: Starter, Pro and Max. Captions are listed from Pro, which costs $34 a month or $20 a month billed yearly. It includes 25 hours of transcription a month and 5 caption presets.

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

When FireCut is the better pick

  • You want silence removal, zooms and chapters in the same tool.
  • You edit in DaVinci Resolve as well as Premiere Pro.
  • You already pay for FireCut Pro. Use its captions first.

When Captions Gen is the better pick

  • You mostly need captions. No monthly plan for one feature.
  • You caption a lot. There's no hour limit, because transcription runs on your computer.
  • You want more looks. 18 presets, and you can save your own.
  • Your footage is private. Nothing is uploaded.

How to switch

  1. Download Captions Gen and run the installer. It needs Windows 10 or 11 and Premiere Pro 25.0 or newer.
  2. Open Premiere Pro and go to Window > UXP Plugins > Captions Gen.
  3. Select the clip with the voice in your timeline.
  1. Pick a look on the Presets tab.
  2. Press Generate.

Tip: You can keep FireCut for cutting. Add captions with Captions Gen as the last step, once the cut is final.

Questions people ask

Is FireCut free?

No. FireCut has a 7-day free trial. Its Premiere Pro plan with captions (Pro) costs $34 a month, or $20 a month billed yearly (checked 22 October 2026).

What is a free alternative to FireCut for captions?

Captions Gen is a free Premiere Pro plugin for Windows. It makes animated, word-by-word captions with no time limit and no watermark. It doesn't cut silences.

Does FireCut upload my audio?

FireCut's plans list transcription hours per month (25 hours on Pro), which points to online transcription. Captions Gen transcribes on your computer with no hour limit.

Can I use FireCut and Captions Gen together?

Yes. Cut with FireCut, then add the captions with Captions Gen. They work on the same timeline.
