Premiere's built-in captions (included with Premiere)

For native subtitles, transcribe the sequence, then Create captions. Speech to Text is included with Premiere and runs locally once the language pack is installed. Current versions offer a Single Word layout and Track Styles for a consistent look. A native subtitle track is a different workflow from the editable animated Motion Graphics that Captions Gen generates. See the built-in captions guide.

Captions Gen (currently free, no time cap, no watermark)

The current free release covers local transcription (Whisper, on your machine), word-by-word karaoke highlighting, 18 animated presets, a live preview, your own saved presets and restyling captions you've already placed. The captions are editable Motion Graphics on your timeline. See product facts, pricing and compatibility.

The old 15-minute trial limit no longer applies. It was removed in Captions Gen 1.1.0, released on 21 October 2026. If an older installed panel still shows that limit, update to the current public version.

  1. Download Captions Gen. The current free Lemon Squeezy checkout asks for your email address; no payment card is needed.
  2. Run the installer. It sets up the engine, the Premiere Pro panel and the free fonts the presets use.
  3. In Premiere Pro, open Window, UXP Plugins, Captions Gen, select your clip, pick a preset and press Generate.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer. The panel uses Adobe's UXP plugin platform, so Premiere Pro 2024 and versions earlier than 25.6 are not supported.

Transcription runs locally on your CPU; a dedicated GPU is not required for transcription. Choose Base for faster processing, Small for a balance of speed and accuracy, or Turbo for the default model. Processing time depends on your CPU, available memory, clip length and model. The engine downloads the built-in speech models at initial startup and caches them; allow at least 3 GB free for models, plus space for the app and your projects. Your audio is not uploaded for transcription. Follow Adobe's CPU, RAM, GPU and Windows requirements for your Premiere version, with additional memory available for the speech model. Questions people ask

Is there a free animated caption plugin for Premiere Pro?

Yes. Captions Gen is currently free for Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on 64-bit Windows supported by your Premiere version. It creates editable animated captions with local transcription, no watermark and no transcription time cap. Adobe Premiere Pro is a separate paid subscription.

Is Captions Gen a 15-minute trial?

No. The 15-minute limit was removed in version 1.1.0 on 21 October 2026. The current release has no transcription time cap, watermark or subscription. Paid features are planned for the future.

Do I need a card to download Captions Gen?

No. The current free download uses Lemon Squeezy checkout and asks for your email address, without a payment card. See current product facts and download details.

Does Captions Gen upload my footage for transcription?

No. Transcription processes your audio locally on your CPU. Initial speech-model downloads and update checks use the internet. Local transcription does not mean the plugin makes no network requests.

Does Captions Gen work on Mac or older Premiere versions?

The current public release supports Windows and Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer. Mac and Premiere versions before 25.6 are not supported. Your computer must also meet Adobe's requirements for its installed Premiere version.
