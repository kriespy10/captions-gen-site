Choose a free workflow by output

Premiere's built-in Speech to Text is included with a paid Premiere plan and can make native caption tracks. Current versions offer Track Styles and a Single Word layout. That is the starting point when you need native subtitles, SRT or specified closed-caption delivery.

Captions Gen is the graphics route for a moving highlight, pill background or animated caption design. Its 18 built-in presets and saved custom designs do not turn into a native subtitle track. Use the ten-option comparison if you also want to compare other plugins, trials and paid plans.

The old 15-minute limit has been removed

The previous 15-minute trial cap ended with version 1.1.0 on 21 October 2026. The current release has no transcription time cap, watermark or subscription. An older installed panel or an old description can still refer to the previous limit; use the current product and release pages for the public offer.

No time cap does not mean instantaneous processing. Speed depends on the CPU, chosen model, memory and audio length. Generated graphics also add work during playback and export.

Check compatibility and model storage

Premiere requires 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Meet Adobe's requirements for the installed Premiere version, with memory available for the speech model. Older Premiere versions and a public Mac installer are not supported by this release.

Transcription runs on the CPU without GPU acceleration. A dedicated GPU is not required for transcription itself, but Premiere's own graphics requirements still apply. Base is the lightest built-in model, Small offers another balance, and Turbo is the default and largest built-in choice.

Allow at least 3 GB free for built-in models, plus space for the plugin, Adobe apps and projects. Missing models download and are cached for reuse. Internet is used for those downloads and update checks; audio and video are not uploaded for transcription.

Install the Premiere panel

  1. Open the free download checkout and enter an email address for the download link. No payment card is required.
  2. Save your projects and close the Adobe apps. Run the current full Windows setup and choose Premiere Pro, or Both if you also need After Effects.
  3. Keep Creative Cloud Desktop installed and complete the required Adobe installation prompts. Setup installs the selected panels, shared engine and bundled preset fonts.
  4. Reopen Premiere and choose Window > UXP Plugins > Captions Gen.
  5. Wait for the selected speech model to show ready before generating. A panel being open does not mean its model download is complete.

The Windows EXE remains Authenticode-unsigned. Signing of the separate Adobe CEP package does not resolve a Windows Smart App Control block. If setup is blocked, contact support; do not disable protection just to run the download.

Generate and inspect a short sample

Select a suitable timeline speech clip, choose a preset and use Customize to adjust its design. Generate Captions, then check spelling, names, numbers and highlights against the audio. Review a short exported sample before processing a long edit.

Use Apply style to change the generated batch's appearance. After changing caption words, use Update animation where offered and review timing again. Available timing can be retained, while new words may need alignment or estimated timing.

The browser caption designer lets you explore prepared sample text and designs. It does not transcribe files or place graphics into an Adobe project.

The same free setup also offers After Effects

The 1.1.9 installer offers Premiere Pro, After Effects or Both. Both panels share the speech engine and Models folder. After Effects receives native editable caption layers and has been tested with After Effects 2026 on Windows 11; earlier versions are not certified for this release.

See the free After Effects captions plugin and output examples before choosing it. After Effects uses Window > Extensions > Captions Gen. Follow the After Effects setup and first-caption guide for supported source layers and composition-specific steps.

Keep subtitle delivery separate

The plugin does not import SRT, convert native subtitle tracks, translate speech or add automatic B-roll in this public release. If the client needs selectable subtitles or a caption file, keep the native Premiere caption workflow alongside the animated picture.

Questions people ask

Is there a free animated caption plugin for Premiere Pro?

Yes. Captions Gen is currently free for Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on 64-bit Windows supported by your Premiere version. It creates editable animated captions with local transcription, no watermark and no transcription time cap. Adobe Premiere Pro is a separate paid subscription.

Is Captions Gen a 15-minute trial?

No. The 15-minute limit was removed in version 1.1.0 on 21 October 2026. The current release has no transcription time cap, watermark or subscription. Paid features are planned for the future.

Do I need a card to download Captions Gen?

No. The current free download uses Lemon Squeezy checkout and asks for your email address, without a payment card. See current product facts and download details.

Does Captions Gen upload my footage for transcription?

No. Transcription processes your audio locally on your CPU. Initial speech-model downloads and update checks use the internet. Local transcription does not mean the plugin makes no network requests.

Does Captions Gen work on Mac or older Premiere versions?

The current public release supports Windows and Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer. Mac and Premiere versions before 25.6 are not supported. Your computer must also meet Adobe's requirements for its installed Premiere version.

Does the free release also support After Effects?

Yes. The 1.1.9 Windows setup offers Premiere Pro, After Effects, or Both. The panel has been tested with After Effects 2026 on Windows 11; earlier After Effects versions are not certified. Both panels share the engine and Models folder.

Can Captions Gen export SRT or translate captions?

The public release creates editable animated graphics. It does not provide native subtitle-track conversion or translation. Use Premiere's native caption workflow when you need an SRT sidecar or selectable captions.
