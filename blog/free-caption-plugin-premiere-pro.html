Free caption plugins for Premiere Pro

Updated 22 October 2026 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

Quick answer: for plain captions, Premiere's own Speech to Text is free and unlimited. For animated, word-by-word captions, Captions Gen is a free plugin with no time limit and no watermark: it transcribes on your computer and places karaoke-style captions on your timeline in one click. Most other caption plugins are paid, with a trial or a capped free tier.

Premiere's built-in captions (free, unlimited)

Transcribe the sequence, then Create captions. Speech to Text is included with Premiere and runs locally once the language pack is installed. Current versions offer a Single Word layout and Track Styles for a consistent look. What it can't do is highlight the word being spoken or animate words in.

Captions Gen (free, no time limit, no watermark)

Captions Gen, the plugin I make, is free. It covers local transcription (Whisper, on your machine), word-by-word karaoke highlighting, 18 animated presets, a live preview, your own saved presets and restyling captions you've already placed. The captions are editable Motion Graphics on your timeline, not a rendered video.

  1. Download Captions Gen. Checkout is free and only asks for an email address.
  2. Run the installer. It sets up the engine, the Premiere Pro panel and the free fonts the presets use.
  3. In Premiere Pro, open Window, UXP Plugins, Captions Gen, select your clip, pick a preset and press Generate.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 and Premiere Pro 25.0 or newer.

Free tiers of other caption plugins

Plugins that animate captions usually charge, because word-level transcription and the caption templates are the product. Their free route is a trial with a day or minute cap, or exports with a watermark. Read the limit before you build a workflow around it: a watermark on client work is a bad surprise.

The CapCut route

Check the current CapCut plan and the caption features available in your region. If you caption in CapCut and bring the video back into Premiere, the burned-in text can no longer be edited in Premiere.

Common questions

Is Captions Gen really free?

Yes. No time limit, no watermark and no subscription.

Does it upload my footage?

No. Transcription runs on your computer, so the audio never leaves it.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet. Captions Gen is Windows-only for now. On a Mac, use Premiere's own captions or test a paid plugin's trial.
