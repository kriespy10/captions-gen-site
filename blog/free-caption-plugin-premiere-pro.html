Free caption plugins for Premiere Pro

Updated 2026-08-26 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

The most complete free option is already inside Premiere: its own speech-to-text makes static captions at no cost. For animated captions, most plugins are paid, and the free route is a trial or a free tier with a limit. Here is what you get for free from each approach.

Short on time? Captions Gen does animated karaoke captions in Premiere Pro in one click. Try it free (15 minutes of transcription included).

Premiere's built-in captions (free, unlimited)

Transcribe sequence, Create captions, done. Unlimited minutes, runs offline, decent accuracy. Its limit is the look: one static style per track, no word highlighting, no animation.

Free tiers of caption plugins

Plugins that animate captions usually charge, because word-level transcription and the caption templates are the product. Some offer a trial with a minute cap or a watermark. Read the limit before you build a workflow around it; a watermark on client work is a bad surprise.

Captions Gen's free version

Captions Gen has a free version with 15 minutes of transcription and no watermark. Everything else is the full plugin: karaoke highlighting, presets, live preview, restyle. Fifteen minutes is enough for ten to fifteen short clips, so you can decide with real projects. The paid version is a one-time $14.99.

The CapCut route

CapCut's auto captions are free, but you leave Premiere, round-trip the video, and bring back burned-in text you can't edit. Free in money, expensive in time on every revision.

Pick by what you're making

Documentary-style subtitles: Premiere's own tool. Short-form with animated captions: a plugin, tested through its free tier first.
