Premiere Pro Captions Not Showing: 6 Fixes

Updated 2026-08-26 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

Captions exist on the timeline but the Program Monitor shows nothing — or they show while editing and vanish from the export. Both are almost always one hidden toggle. Work down this list; most people are done at fix 1 or 2.

1. The CC toggle in the Program Monitor

The Program Monitor has its own captions display switch: the CC button under the preview (wrench menu on older versions). If it's off, captions never render in preview no matter what the timeline says. Click it, choose your caption track, done. This is the number-one cause.

2. Caption track visibility

Caption tracks have an output toggle like video tracks, in the caption track header on the left of the timeline. Bumped off, everything disappears. Also check the track isn't collapsed so far you're not seeing the caption blocks at all.

3. You have a transcript, not captions

The Text panel's Transcript tab showing text does not mean captions exist. Transcript and captions are separate things — if the Captions tab is empty and there's no caption track on the timeline, you still need to click Create captions.

4. Captions missing from the export

Preview fine, export blank: open the export dialog's Captions tab. If it says None, nothing goes out. Pick Burn captions into video for social (text becomes part of the image) or Create sidecar file for a separate .srt. This tab resets more often than you'd think between exports.

5. Closed captions on a player that hides them

If the track format is CEA-608/708 closed captions, the text only shows when the player's CC feature is on — that's the format working as designed. For always-visible captions on social, use Subtitle format and burn in.

6. Overlapping or zero-length captions

Imported .srt files sometimes carry overlapping timecodes or zero-duration entries, which render as flicker or nothing. Scrub the caption track for suspiciously thin blocks; retiming or deleting them fixes it.

Still nothing?

Restart Premiere with the caption track visible and CC on — the Program Monitor occasionally needs the nudge after toggling. And if captions show in preview but not in your upload, re-check fix 4: nine times out of ten, the export tab quietly said None.
