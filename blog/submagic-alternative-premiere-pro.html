How Submagic works

Submagic is a website, not a Premiere Pro plugin. The workflow looks like this:

  1. Export your video from your editor.
  2. Upload it to Submagic.
  3. Pick a caption style and fix the words.
  4. Download the video with the captions burned in.
  5. Import it back into Premiere if you want to keep editing.

Submagic also advertises SRT export. An SRT can bring text and timings into Premiere's native caption workflow, but it doesn't transfer animated highlights, backgrounds or bounce effects. A downloaded video contains the rendered animation in its picture.

What does the handoff preserve?

Choose the deliverable before choosing a caption style. A rendered video preserves the visible result, while SRT preserves editable words and times. Neither imports the web editor's animated design as native Premiere graphics.

Why editors look for an alternative

  • The rendered-video round trip. Changes to the underlying cut may require another export and upload. An SRT handoff avoids baking in the text, but loses the animation.
  • Length caps. Longer videos need a bigger plan.
  • Credits. Captions are one part of the editing bill: uploads and additional AI operations use the monthly credit pool.
  • Client footage. Some clients don't want their footage uploaded to another service.
  • Graphic edits. A typo in a rendered animated video needs another render. An SRT can be edited as native caption text, with a separate styling workflow.

When Submagic is the better pick

  • You want to work in a browser, or you edit on a Mac where Captions Gen has no public installer.
  • You want one tool that also adds B-roll, emojis and sound effects to a talking-head clip.

Step by step

  1. Get the free Windows setup directly, with no email address or payment card required. Save your work, close the Adobe apps, then choose Premiere Pro in setup. You need Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, Creative Cloud Desktop and Adobe installation permissions.
  2. Reopen Premiere Pro and go to Window > UXP Plugins > Captions Gen. Let any missing speech models download before generating; allow at least 3 GB for the built-in models. Later transcription uses the cached models on your CPU.
  3. Select the clip with the voice in your timeline.

Use the full Setup for a first installation. Check the current product requirements and 1.1.9 release notes if you're updating an existing installation.

  1. Pick a preset on the Presets tab.
  2. Press Generate Captions. Review names and difficult phrases before placing graphics if transcript review is enabled.

Try a short duplicate sequence before replacing an existing workflow. Generate from the original speech clip rather than a downloaded video that already contains visible captions, or you can end up with two caption layers in the picture.

For an appearance change, choose a preset and use Apply style. Changing grouping can require transcription again. If the underlying cut changes, recheck or regenerate the affected section.

Tip: Correct text in the caption editor or Premiere's Properties, then use Update animation when offered. Review the new highlights against the voice; a corrected or added word can receive estimated timing.

The 1.1.9 setup also offers an After Effects panel, tested with After Effects 2026 on Windows 11. It creates native editable caption layers; use the After Effects editing guide for its controls. There is no public Mac installer or translation feature.

Questions people ask

Is Submagic free?

Submagic offers a trial for uploading and editing three short videos; its help page says no-watermark downloads require a subscription. Its official help lists monthly US-dollar plans from $19 per member. Credits, billing period and regional pricing affect the quote. Sources reviewed 9 November 2026.

Is there a free Submagic alternative without a watermark?

Captions Gen is currently free without a watermark or transcription time limit for Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows. The installer downloads directly, with no email address or payment card required. Adobe Premiere Pro is separate and paid.

Does Submagic work inside Premiere Pro?

Submagic's editor runs in the browser rather than as a Premiere panel. You upload media and download the result. Submagic also advertises SRT export for text and timing; that file doesn't carry its animated graphic appearance.

Can I get Submagic-style captions in Premiere Pro?

You can make similar comic, bold-highlight and pill-background looks with Captions Gen's Beast Comic, Bold Impact and Karaoke Pill presets. These are Captions Gen designs, not copies of Submagic templates, and they remain editable graphics on the Premiere timeline.

Is my footage uploaded with Captions Gen?

Captions Gen doesn't upload audio or video for transcription. Faster Whisper runs locally on the CPU. Missing speech models download and are cached; update checks also use the internet.
