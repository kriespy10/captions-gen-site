Word-by-word captions in After Effects: layout or highlight?

Published 9 November 2026 · by Kris, maker of Captions Gen

After Effects guide. Free plugin, output examples and compatibility. Working in Premiere Pro? Choose a Premiere caption workflow.

For one word on screen at a time, set Captions Gen's Min words and Max words to 1. For a phrase that stays visible while the spoken word changes color or gains a pill, keep several words per caption and enable Highlight words as spoken. Both can look like word-by-word captions, but grouping and highlighting solve different problems.

This guide covers the public 1.1.9 After Effects panel for Windows. Install it and create a first batch with the AE setup guide.

For a sentence the viewer needs to read ahead, a short phrase can provide more context than one isolated word. For a deliberate single-word emphasis, the one-word layout may fit. Try the actual speech rather than choosing solely from a still image.

Build a one-word layout

  1. Select the supported speech layer in your composition.
  2. Choose a preset, then open Customize > Caption text.
  3. Set Min words to 1 and Max words to 1.
  4. Choose text case, punctuation and line count for the design.
  5. Generate captions and listen through several word changes, including pauses.

A one-word grouping does not by itself add karaoke color, a pill or a bouncing entrance. Choose those separately under Design. Start with a restrained entrance so a very short word is not spent entirely inside its animation.

Grouping changes after a batch exists can regenerate captions through Apply style using saved source history. Save the project before changing a long batch, and inspect the new phrase boundaries afterward. Do not promise that changing Max words only restyles existing text layers without another source pass.

Keep a phrase visible with moving word highlights

  1. Choose Karaoke or Karaoke Pill as a starting preset.
  2. In Caption text, keep several words in each group. The released karaoke path supports up to 8 words per caption.
  3. Open the Karaoke group under Design and enable Highlight words as spoken.
  4. Choose Color swap or Pill background, then set the highlight color and related controls.
  5. Preview with the source audio, especially names, contractions and words around pauses.

The general Max words slider extends beyond eight, but that is not the karaoke highlight capacity. The panel also uses character, duration and line limits when grouping. A maximum is a limit, not a guarantee that every caption contains that exact number of words.

Use the After Effects mode in the browser demo to compare color and pill designs on prepared sample text. It cannot demonstrate recognition quality for your own audio, and its sample word timings are estimated.

How this differs from native text animation

After Effects also has native text animators and selectors for targeting parts of a text layer. Adobe's text animation examples include selecting a specific word. That gives you manual animation control; selecting a word does not itself transcribe speech or create verified spoken-word timing.

Captions Gen supplies a generated caption workflow with editable precompositions. You do not need to build a separate text layer for every word just to use its phrase highlight. If you manually change generated Source Text afterward, follow the panel's editing and timing rules so the rig and its wording stay consistent.

Review readability and corrections together

Watch the phrase over both bright and dark frames. Check that the active word is distinguishable and that a pill does not collide with other text. For long words, adjust the design and grouping instead of assuming a fixed word count will fit every shot.

Correct words in the Captions tab and use Save caption. Saving updates animation automatically. For a manual Source Text change, use Update animation when offered. New words can need estimated timing if original-audio alignment is unavailable; see editing captions and recovering timing.

Render a short sample and watch it with sound at the intended viewing size. These graphics become part of the picture, not an SRT file or a selectable subtitle stream. If a caption is absent rather than mistimed, use the AE visibility and export checklist.

Questions people ask

How do I show only one word at a time?

In Captions Gen Customize > Caption text, set Min words and Max words to 1 before generation. That changes the grouping. Check the result against speech and choose an entrance/exit that does not obscure short words.

How do I keep a phrase visible and highlight each spoken word?

Keep several words per caption and enable Highlight words as spoken in the Karaoke design group. Choose Color swap or Pill background, then review the word timing against the audio.

How many words can a karaoke caption contain?

The public panel limits karaoke highlights to up to eight words per caption. Its broader Max words control is a grouping option, not a promise to highlight 36 words in one karaoke caption.

Do I need a separate layer for every highlighted word?

Not in this generated workflow. Captions Gen creates editable phrase precompositions and animates their words. A one-word layout instead makes individual words the grouping units.
