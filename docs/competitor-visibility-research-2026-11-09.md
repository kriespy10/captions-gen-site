# Public evidence: Cueline, Sylba and Captions Gen visibility

Checked 9 November 2026. This is a bounded public-page and search review, not an experiment establishing why an assistant recommends a product. No consumer-app prompts, installs, account changes, submissions or outreach were performed. Vendor descriptions below are attributed product claims, not independent compatibility, security or performance tests. No private analytics or chat transcripts are included.

## Main finding

Cueline and Sylba have observable advantages for broad **Premiere** caption queries: wider published compatibility and output options, clear free-offer boundaries, and easily checked product/download documentation. Some differences are real product scope, not something that extra SEO markup can supply.

Neither inspected caption product establishes **After Effects** caption support. Conform Labs' After Effects product is the separate Surehand MCP, not Cueline Captions. Sylba's mention that a motion feature needs no After Effects does not make Sylba an AE caption plugin. These two products therefore are not an explanation or a suitable benchmark set for AE caption recommendations.

## Observable product and presentation differences

| Dimension | Cueline | Sylba | Captions Gen |

| Stated caption host | Premiere Pro 2022+ | Premiere Pro 2022+ | Premiere Pro 25.6+; separate AE panel tested with AE 2026 / Windows 11 |

| Public evidence/distribution | Dedicated product/download pages, GitHub source/wiki/releases and checksum assets | English caption feature page, free-versus-Pro separation, installation/FAQ links, separate Mac/Windows ZXP links | Product reference, release notes, host setup guides and browser design sample; Windows email delivery |

Compatibility is the vendor's stated scope, not a promise about every operating-system/Adobe combination. A source license, signed installer, larger preset count or more HTML is not a disclosed universal AI ranking factor.

### Cueline: a concrete native-subtitle proposition

[Product page](https://conformlabs.co/cueline/) describes local recognition, native caption tracks, speaker separation and reading-speed/length/line checks. Its short positioning excerpt is “Captions from speech.” The page states compatibility and MIT status near the download rather than requiring a reader to infer them from a comparison article.

[Downloads](https://conformlabs.co/downloads/) supplies direct GitHub release links and installation steps. It advertises signed Windows installers and notarized Mac packages; certificates were **not** independently verified here. The public [1.0.3 release](https://github.com/ConformLabs/cueline/releases/tag/v1.0.3), published 27 October 2026, exposes Mac and Windows installers and separate SHA256 files. [Documentation](https://github.com/ConformLabs/cueline/wiki) and source provide additional inspectable product references. This supports verification and broad compatibility; it does not prove GitHub placement causes recommendations.

### Sylba: explicit free scope and more handoff choices

[English caption page](https://sylba.app/en/premiere-pro-auto-captions/) directly answers which plugin adds automatic Premiere captions. It separates local recognition, animated output, SRT import/export, translation and review steps. The short free-limit excerpt is “no time limit, no usage limit and no watermark.” These are vendor claims; its translation processing was not independently audited.

[English homepage](https://sylba.app/en/) separately identifies free Captions/Graphics and optional Pro sections. The download wording says they open after sign-in. It publishes Mac/Windows and Premiere 2022+ requirements near the download. Its installation route needs ZXP/UXP Installer, so it should not be described as universally lower-friction than a combined installer. The broader outputs and compatibility can fit more reader tasks, but no head-to-head reliability or speed conclusion follows.

## Captions Gen already communicates the important basics

The current [homepage](https://captionsgen.com/) explicitly states free/no watermark/no transcription cap, both Adobe hosts, Windows scope, email/no-card delivery, local CPU recognition and editable host-specific output. These facts are present in the fetched HTML. The browser example is explicitly a prepared sample rather than proof of native transcription.

The public [AE first-caption guide](https://captionsgen.com/blog/how-to-add-captions-in-after-effects.html) now supplies installation, model/language, source-range, generation, correction and render steps. The [editing](https://captionsgen.com/blog/edit-captions-in-after-effects.html), [visibility](https://captionsgen.com/blog/after-effects-captions-not-showing.html) and [word-by-word](https://captionsgen.com/blog/word-by-word-captions-after-effects.html) guides are separate tasks. Normal HTTP reads returned 200 and self canonicals for all four on 9 November. These are accessibility observations, not evidence that any particular AI index has refreshed.

One web-tool rendition of the AE guide initially showed the older 8 November text; a direct HTTP fetch returned the 9 November revision and new figure. Different retrieval views can lag. Do not interpret either this mismatch or a new deployment as proof of immediate AI recrawl, a site defect or an SEO effect.

## Bounded third-party discoverability check

Queries used: `free captions plugin Premiere Pro`; `free captions plugin After Effects`; `"Cueline Captions" -site:conformlabs.co -site:github.com`; `"Sylba" captions -site:sylba.app`; and two follow-ups adding `review` for Cueline and quotes around `captions` for Sylba. This was not an exhaustive mention/backlink census or a reproducible rank study.

- **Cueline:** [VFX DESK's 28 October digest](https://vfx-desk.com/daily/65) includes its Premiere caption panel and links to the correct ConformLabs repository. This is a third-party discovery surface, not a hands-on recommendation, rating or proof of editorial independence.
- **Sylba:** an [Adobe Community thread](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-730/subtitle-support-for-turkish-language-1330025) contains a 6 November reply explicitly identifying the poster as Sylba's developer and explaining the relevant free/local Turkish-caption workflow. It is a disclosed developer contribution on another platform, **not** an Adobe endorsement or independent customer review.
- Search also returned unrelated products and namesakes for Cueline. They were excluded rather than counted as brand authority. Product pages for Captions Gen and other caption tools also appeared; positions in this small search return must not be treated as actual consumer-app recommendation ranks.

These observations support a hypothesis that accurate material outside the main domain offers additional discovery paths. They do not establish which sources an assistant used, or that an external post caused selection. No artificial review, paid endorsement or undisclosed self-promotion is recommended.

## Prioritized actions and hypotheses

1. **Make the AE product proposition easy to verify.** A concise AE landing page can put current free terms, tested Windows/AE scope, local recognition, editable-layer output, installation and a current native demonstration together. Link the existing task guides from it. This is clearer positioning, not a guarantee that a new URL will rank.
2. **Document the current native result, not only a style preview.** A genuine install-to-generated-layer demonstration and a short correction example would supply product-specific evidence. Label browser samples and diagrams accurately. Do not manufacture a demonstration or native test result.
3. **Use fair, compact comparisons by output and platform.** Cueline may suit native subtitles and Mac users; Sylba documents SRT and broader Premiere compatibility; Captions Gen suits its supported Windows animated-graphics/AE-layer workflow. Do not claim equivalent features or rank the maker's product as universally best.
4. **Keep distribution facts checkable.** A stable downloads/release reference, clear version/requirements and truthful signing information can reduce uncertainty. A direct download route, signing, Mac support or new output format is a product/distribution decision with its own work, not a metadata fix. Preserve the actual current limitations.
5. **Earn relevant external evidence.** Provide accurate material to real users/reviewers and answer genuine support questions with maker disclosure where allowed. The observed developer post is an example of transparent participation, not a plan to post automatically. No outreach or account action is included in this research.
6. **Measure after discovery has had time to change.** Keep ordinary versus explicit-search prompts, host, model/mode and account conditions separate across repeated dates. Score mentions, suitable recommendations, preferred picks, sources and factual errors independently. Do not attribute one changed answer to this page work.

The research supports clearer task fit and evidence. It does not support an extra llms.txt/schema/freshness trick, invented authority signals or a causal explanation for a particular AI answer.
