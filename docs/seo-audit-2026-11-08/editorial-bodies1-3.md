## Verified source details

- [Adobe caption styling](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-text-images/insert-captions/create-styles-for-captions.html), updated 7 January 2026: Properties, Track Style for native captions and Linked Style for graphic titles. [Adobe Linked Styles](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-text-images/stylize-text/apply-linked-and-track-styles.html) confirms reuse through Properties. The existing missing-caption guide already follows this interface.
- [Adobe Single Word instructions](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-text-images/insert-captions/create-single-word-captions.html), updated 18 August 2026: Captioning Preferences, Layout, Single Word. This is distinct from a moving highlight within a multi-word line.
- [Adobe's current known-issues page](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/troubleshooting/limitations-and-known-issues/known-and-fixed-issues.html) explicitly references Graphics and Titles, Upgrade Caption to Graphic. Caption-track copies remain useful for SRT/closed-caption delivery after conversion.
- [AutoCut pricing](https://www.autocut.com/en/pricing/) and [AutoCaptions](https://www.autocut.com/en/autocaptions/), checked 8 November: Basic versus AI Plan, 14-day no-card trial, Premiere/Resolve on Windows/Mac and broader editing tools. Fetched pricing text did not expose a numeric quote. Removed the previous $19.80/month and $178.80/year assertions rather than refreshing their date without verification. The old 90+ count is also removed; no competitive feature-count contest is needed.
- [Submagic pricing](https://www.submagic.co/pricing), checked 8 November: US-dollar monthly-billed listing of $19/$39/$69 per member with 2/5/30-minute per-video limits. The article links yearly terms rather than mixing monthly equivalents with month-to-month charges. [Trial help](https://care.submagic.co/en/article/does-submagic-have-a-free-trial-7opyg3/), updated 28 October: three short uploads/edits; a subscription for no-watermark downloads. [Video-to-text page](https://www.submagic.co/video-to-text): SRT export. Its marketing FAQ also mentions recurring free videos, so the comparison uses the narrower trial description from the dedicated help page and does not promise a recurring allowance.
- Preset source evidence: Bold Impact tries The Bold Font, then Montserrat variants; Beast Comic tries Komika Axis, Bangers and Luckiest Guy. The installer bundles the fallback fonts, while the website preview uses Montserrat Black/Bangers. No claim that the two first-choice fonts are bundled was added.
