Privacy

Updated 9 November 2026

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages bring visitors to captionsgen.com and whether visitors click our download button. The Google Analytics tag only loads after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you decline or leave the choice unanswered, this website sends no Google Analytics requests.

When allowed, analytics sends Google information about the pages you visit, referral and campaign sources, browser and device information, and website interactions. Google uses analytics cookies to associate visits. We do not enable Google advertising signals or advertising personalization, and our custom button events contain no email, payment details, audio, or video.

After you allow analytics, Google's cross-domain settings for this site and our Lemon Squeezy checkout can add a short-lived _gl parameter to checkout links. This passes analytics identifiers so a consented website visit can be associated with checkout activity. Browser settings, consent and checkout behavior can limit that measurement. A download-button click does not by itself confirm a completed download or purchase.

We keep your allow or decline preference in your browser for 180 days. The analytics cookies we set also expire after 180 days. You can change your choice using . Choosing Decline after allowing analytics removes this website’s Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop collection. Data already sent to Google is not removed by that choice.

See Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles information and Google’s browser opt-out tool for an additional opt-out option.

Homepage demo and saved presets

The homepage demo uses prepared sample text in your browser. Generated sample captions, your caption edits, Undo history and example-model assignments stay in page memory and reset when you reload the page or choose Reset demo. They are not saved with a design preset. These demo actions do not read your media, download speech models, transcribe audio or connect to your Adobe applications.

If you save or import a design preset, its name and style settings, including font metadata, stay in this browser’s local storage for your next visit. Your main-preset choice is also kept here. The demo does not upload these designs. Use a saved preset’s menu under Your presets to delete it from this browser. Export downloads a preset file to your computer; Reset demo keeps saved presets.

Checkout and purchases

Our download button opens Lemon Squeezy’s hosted checkout, where you enter your email to get the free download. Lemon Squeezy processes checkout information, payments, receipts, and licensing under its own privacy policy. This website’s analytics preference applies to captionsgen.com; the checkout is a separate service.

Local transcription in Premiere Pro and After Effects

Captions Gen processes speech locally on your CPU in both Adobe applications. Audio and video are not uploaded for transcription. Both Windows panels use the same engine and Models folder. Missing model downloads, update checks and legacy license checks use the internet. Optional reports and usage statistics are separate flows described below.

Optional usage stats in the plugin

The first time you open the panel, it asks whether you want to send usage stats. Events with a random install ID are sent only if you choose “Yes”. The separate install and running counts below can be sent before you choose. You can change the usage choice in the panel’s Settings.

When enabled, usage stats include a random install ID created on your computer, the engine version, operating system, whether the copy is licensed, and which tracked steps happened, such as opening the panel, transcribing or placing captions. The payload does not include your name, email, audio, video or transcript. We use it to understand where people get stuck. The current public plugin supports Windows.

Turning stats off stops future stats, clears your local install ID and requests deletion of its stored data. We do not store IP addresses with usage stats. Stored stats expire about 13 months after their last update.

Anonymous install counts

The shared background engine can send an “installed” signal once after installation and a “running” signal at most once a day before you make a usage-stats choice. These signals contain no install ID; we keep daily totals rather than per-install records. Choosing “No thanks” stops these counts too.

Email updates

You can sign up for email updates in the plugin. We only add you if you type your email address and press Subscribe; the plugin works the same without it. We store your email address, when and where you signed up, the plugin version, and which version of the sign-up text you agreed to. We use them only to send you news about Captions Gen updates, new features, new caption styles and other video tools from the maker of Captions Gen, a few times a year.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe in the plugin’s Settings or by emailing us. Unsubscribing deletes your address from our list. We may use an email delivery service to send these emails.

Bug reports and feature requests

If you press Send on a bug report or feature request, we receive your message, the optional email address you enter, engine and panel versions, the Adobe application and its version, operating system and license-status flag. After Effects reports identify the After Effects host and version; Premiere reports identify Premiere. Feature requests do not include the engine log.

Bug reports automatically include a recent engine-log excerpt, up to 20,000 characters. The sender redacts some identifiers, including the Windows home-folder username, email addresses and license-like IDs. That redaction does not guarantee removal of every file name or project detail. Review the message and avoid including sensitive project information when reporting a problem. Reports are used to respond and fix problems, and deleted when no longer needed.

Usage stats, install counts, email sign-ups, and reports are stored on Cloudflare Workers, and reports are emailed to us through Resend.
