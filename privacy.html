Privacy

Updated 10 November 2026

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages bring visitors to captionsgen.com and whether visitors click our download button. The Google Analytics tag only loads after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you decline or leave the choice unanswered, this website sends no Google Analytics requests.

When allowed, analytics sends Google information about the pages you visit, referral and campaign sources, browser and device information, and website interactions. Google uses analytics cookies to associate visits. We do not enable Google advertising signals or advertising personalization, and our custom button events contain no email, payment details, audio, or video.

Consented download-button events identify where the link appeared on the page and include the installer version when it is present in a direct asset link. They contain no email address or newsletter information. A click does not confirm a completed download or installation. Browser settings, consent and blockers can limit Google Analytics measurement.

We keep your allow or decline preference in your browser for 180 days. The analytics cookies we set also expire after 180 days. You can change your choice using . Choosing Decline after allowing analytics removes this website’s Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop collection. Data already sent to Google is not removed by that choice.

See Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles information and Google’s browser opt-out tool for an additional opt-out option.

Homepage demo and saved presets

The homepage demo uses prepared sample text in your browser. Generated sample captions, your caption edits, Undo history and example-model assignments stay in page memory and reset when you reload the page or choose Reset demo. They are not saved with a design preset. These demo actions do not read your media, download speech models, transcribe audio or connect to your Adobe applications.

If you save or import a design preset, its name and style settings, including font metadata, stay in this browser’s local storage for your next visit. Your main-preset choice is also kept here. The demo does not upload these designs. Use a saved preset’s menu under Your presets to delete it from this browser. Export downloads a preset file to your computer; Reset demo keeps saved presets.

Direct installer downloads

Download buttons pass through our download endpoint and redirect to the Windows installer hosted on GitHub Releases. No email address, account, payment card or newsletter signup is required. GitHub handles installer requests under its privacy statement.

Our endpoint adds to a daily and calendar-month request total. This does not require Google Analytics or plugin usage consent. These totals count requests, including repeats; they do not confirm completed transfers, installations or individual people. We also read GitHub’s aggregate installer counts. Neither source includes historical Lemon Squeezy downloads.

Download browser estimates

To estimate how many browsers download each day and calendar month, our download endpoint uses a first-party cookie named cg_download_counts. It contains only the last download date and month, such as 2026-11-10.2026-11, and expires after 35 days. It contains no random visitor ID. We store aggregate totals, not a list of browsers, cookie values, IP addresses or email addresses.

These estimates are separate from optional Google Analytics. You can view or change your download-counting preference. Turning browser estimates off clears the date cookie and saves an off preference for 35 days. Request totals still increase. Clearing or blocking cookies, using another browser, simultaneous requests and automated downloads can affect the estimates. They are not an exact count of people.

Distribution totals are kept for up to 400 days. Our hosting providers necessarily receive connection information when serving requests; our measurement database does not retain IP addresses or use device fingerprints.

Earlier purchases and licenses remain with Lemon Squeezy under its privacy policy.

Local transcription in Premiere Pro and After Effects

Captions Gen processes speech locally on your CPU in both Adobe applications. Audio and video are not uploaded for transcription. Both Windows panels use the same engine and Models folder. Missing model downloads, update checks and legacy license checks use the internet. Optional reports and usage statistics are separate flows described below.

Optional usage stats in the plugin

The first time you open the panel, it asks whether you want to send usage stats. Events with a random usage ID are sent only if you choose “Yes”. The separate installation registration in version 1.1.10 does not require optional usage consent. You can change the usage choice in the panel’s Settings.

When enabled, usage stats include a random usage ID created on your computer, the engine version, operating system, whether the copy is licensed, and which tracked steps happened, such as opening the panel, transcribing or placing captions. The payload does not include your name, email, audio, video or transcript. We use it to understand where people get stuck. The current public plugin supports Windows.

Turning stats off stops future stats, clears your local usage ID and requests deletion of its stored data. We do not store IP addresses with usage stats. Stored stats expire about 13 months after their last update.

Legacy install counts in version 1.1.9 and earlier

Until you install the update, older plugins can send an “installed” signal once after installation and a “running” signal at most once a day before you make a usage-stats choice. These signals contain no usage ID; we keep daily totals rather than per-install records. Choosing “No thanks” stops these legacy counts too. The new session measurement only starts after installing version 1.1.10 and allowing usage stats; upgrading preserves earlier opt-out choices.

Installation and session measurement in version 1.1.10

Captions Gen 1.1.10 separates a basic installation count from optional usage statistics. On a fresh installation, the engine sends one installation receipt when it first starts successfully. The receipt contains a random event number and the first-start time. It contains no usage ID, hardware identifier, email address, media or project information. The event number is retained on the server for up to 90 days to prevent retries from counting twice, then removed; the daily and calendar-month totals remain for up to 400 days.

After acknowledgement, your computer keeps only an installation-counted marker. Ordinary reinstalls that keep the app data do not count again. Removing the app data, using another Windows account or another computer can create another count. Offline or blocked requests may be missed. This measures registered installations, not verified unique people. Existing installations are not reclassified as new, and earlier opt-out choices are preserved.

In version 1.1.10, usage sessions require “Allow usage stats” in the plugin. A session begins with a panel opening, transcription or caption placement after at least 30 minutes without one of those actions, or on the first such action of a new UTC day. Starting the background engine alone does not create a session. Optional session records contain a random usage ID, a random session number and its start time, and are retained for up to 400 days. Daily and monthly active-device figures cover only this opted-in sample.

Choosing “No usage stats” stops optional events and sessions and requests deletion of that usage history. Failed deletion requests are retried when the engine next starts. A hash of the retired random usage ID may be retained for 90 days solely to reject delayed events. The separate installation receipt does not contain or share that usage ID or a newsletter address. The installer explains the separate basic count before installation; it does not enable optional usage sessions.

Email updates

You can optionally sign up for email updates on this website or in the plugin. We only add you if you type your email address and press Subscribe; downloading and using the plugin work the same without it. We store your email address, when and where you signed up, the plugin version, and which version of the sign-up text you agreed to. We use them only to send you news about Captions Gen updates, new features, new caption styles and other video tools from the maker of Captions Gen, a few times a year.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe in the plugin’s Settings or by emailing us. Unsubscribing deletes your address from our list. We may use an email delivery service to send these emails.

Bug reports and feature requests

If you press Send on a bug report or feature request, we receive your message, the optional email address you enter, engine and panel versions, the Adobe application and its version, operating system and license-status flag. After Effects reports identify the After Effects host and version; Premiere reports identify Premiere. Feature requests do not include the engine log.

Bug reports automatically include a recent engine-log excerpt, up to 20,000 characters. The sender redacts some identifiers, including the Windows home-folder username, email addresses and license-like IDs. That redaction does not guarantee removal of every file name or project detail. Review the message and avoid including sensitive project information when reporting a problem. Reports are used to respond and fix problems, and deleted when no longer needed.

Usage stats, install counts, email sign-ups, and reports are stored on Cloudflare Workers, and reports are emailed to us through Resend.
