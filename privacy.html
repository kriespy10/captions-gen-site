Privacy

Updated 20 October 2026

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages bring visitors to captionsgen.com and whether visitors click our free trial or buy buttons. The Google Analytics tag only loads after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you decline or leave the choice unanswered, this website sends no Google Analytics requests.

When allowed, analytics sends Google information about the pages you visit, referral and campaign sources, browser and device information, and website interactions. Google uses analytics cookies to associate visits. We do not enable Google advertising signals or advertising personalization, and our custom button events contain no email, payment details, audio, or video.

We keep your allow or decline preference in your browser for 180 days. The analytics cookies we set also expire after 180 days. You can change your choice using . Choosing Decline after allowing analytics removes this website’s Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop collection. Data already sent to Google is not removed by that choice.

See Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles information and Google’s browser opt-out tool for an additional opt-out option.

Saved demo presets

If you save a design in the homepage demo, its name and style settings stay in your browser for your next visit. The demo does not upload saved designs. Open Presets, choose Mine, and use a preset’s delete button to remove it from this browser.

Checkout and purchases

Our trial and buy buttons open Lemon Squeezy’s hosted checkout. Lemon Squeezy processes checkout information, payments, receipts, and licensing under its own privacy policy. This website’s analytics preference applies to captionsgen.com; the checkout is a separate service.

Your footage stays on your machine

The Captions Gen Premiere Pro plugin transcribes locally. It never uploads your audio, video, transcripts, file names, or anything from your projects.

Optional usage stats in the plugin

The first time you open the plugin, it asks whether you want to send anonymous usage stats. Nothing is sent unless you choose “Yes”. You can turn this on or off at any time in the plugin’s Settings.

When on, the plugin sends a random install ID that is created on your computer and not linked to your name, email, or license, together with the plugin version, your operating system (Windows or macOS), whether the copy is licensed, and which of these steps happened that day: opening the panel, transcribing, placing captions, clicking Buy, and activating a license. We use this only to see where people get stuck so we can improve the plugin.

Turning stats off stops sending and deletes the data stored for your install ID. We do not store IP addresses with usage stats, and stored stats are deleted automatically after 13 months.

Anonymous install counts

The plugin’s background engine sends an “installed” signal once after installation and a “running” signal at most once a day, with no ID or other details. We keep only a total count per day, so these signals cannot be linked to you or to each other. Choosing “No thanks” to usage stats stops these signals too.

Email updates

You can sign up for email updates in the plugin. We only add you if you type your email address and press Subscribe; the plugin works the same without it. We store your email address, when and where you signed up, the plugin version, and which version of the sign-up text you agreed to. We use them only to send you news about Captions Gen updates, new caption styles, Pro, and other video tools from the maker of Captions Gen, a few times a year.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe in the plugin’s Settings or by emailing us. Unsubscribing deletes your address from our list. We may use an email delivery service to send these emails.

Bug reports and feature requests

If you send a bug report or feature request from the plugin, we receive your message, the email address you enter (optional), the plugin, Premiere Pro and operating system versions, and for bug reports the last lines of the engine log. We use these only to answer you and fix problems, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Usage stats, install counts, email sign-ups, and reports are stored on Cloudflare Workers, and reports are emailed to us through Resend.
