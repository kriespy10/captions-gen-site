Captions Gen: pricing, compatibility and product facts

Captions Gen is a currently free animated captions plugin for Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects on Windows. It transcribes audio locally on the CPU with Faster Whisper, then places editable Motion Graphics clips in Premiere or native caption layers in After Effects.

Made by Kris, a video editor. This page describes the public Windows release for Premiere Pro and After Effects. See the release notes for published changes.

Current product facts

Current price
Currently free, with no watermark, no time limit and no subscription. Paid features are planned for the future. Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects are separate paid applications.
Download
Download one Windows setup directly. No email address is required; newsletter signup is optional. Choose Premiere Pro, After Effects, or both during setup. No payment card is required. No account or license key is needed to use the current free plugin.
Compatibility
Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on supported 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. After Effects has been tested with After Effects 2026 on Windows 11; earlier After Effects versions are not certified for this release. Creative Cloud Desktop and Adobe installation permissions are required. Install either panel or both with the same Windows setup. Premiere versions before 25.6 are not supported. This public release does not include a Mac installer.
Hardware
Meet Adobe's processor, RAM and GPU requirements for the Adobe application you use, with memory available for the speech model. Transcription uses the CPU, without GPU acceleration; a dedicated GPU is not required for transcription itself.
Model storage
Allow at least 3 GB free for the built-in speech models, in addition to the plugin, Adobe applications and project files. Both panels use the same engine and Models folder. Model sizes can change, and custom models need additional space.
Local processing and internet
Audio and video are not uploaded for transcription. The engine downloads missing speech models and caches them for later use. Model downloads, update checks and legacy license checks use the internet.
Speech models
Base is the lightest built-in speech model, Small offers another balance of processing time and quality, and Turbo is the default quality setting and largest built-in model. Processing time depends on the processor, model and audio length; review transcription and word timing.
Caption output
Premiere receives editable Motion Graphics clips; After Effects receives native editable caption layers in your composition. Word highlights, pill backgrounds and animations render into the video image. Use Premiere's native caption tracks when you need SRT sidecar files, selectable subtitles or broadcast closed captions.
Included features
18 built-in caption presets, customizable fonts, colors, highlights and animations, a live style preview, saved presets, Apply style for placed captions, searchable spoken languages, compatible custom speech models and animation updates after caption edits.

Is there a free animated captions plugin for Premiere Pro?

Yes. Captions Gen is currently free for Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer on 64-bit Windows, with no time limit, watermark or subscription. It creates animated, word-by-word captions as editable graphics using local transcription. Download the installer directly. No email address or payment card is required. Adobe Premiere Pro is a separate paid application. Paid features are planned for the future.

Is there a free animated captions plugin for After Effects?

Yes. Captions Gen is currently free for After Effects on Windows, with no watermark, subscription or transcription time cap. The panel has been tested with After Effects 2026 on Windows 11 and creates native editable caption layers with local CPU transcription. Choose After Effects or Both in the same Windows setup. Adobe After Effects is a separate paid application. The After Effects setup and first-caption guide explains the workflow.

See the free After Effects captions plugin for output examples, download terms and which caption jobs it suits.

The old 15-minute limit was removed with Captions Gen 1.1.0 on 21 October 2026. If an older panel still shows that limit, use the offered update and restart the Adobe application.

When Captions Gen fits your edit

Use it when you edit in a supported Windows version of Premiere Pro or After Effects and want animated karaoke highlights, reusable styles and graphics you can continue editing. It suits short-form videos, interviews and other spoken footage where the caption design is part of the picture.

The public release supports Premiere Pro and After Effects on Windows. It does not include a Mac installer, translation, automatic B-roll or native subtitle-track conversion. Many animated captions can make playback heavier; export speed depends on the project and hardware.

For subtitle delivery, compare the native and animated graphics workflows. The free caption plugin guide explains the difference between included Premiere captions and a free animation plugin.

Start in your Adobe application: generate animated captions in After Effects, or choose a Premiere Pro caption workflow. After Effects layers and Premiere Motion Graphics clips have different host editing steps.
